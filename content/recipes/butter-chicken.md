## Marinade

- <span class="ingredient-amount">1 cup</span> full-fat yogurt (240g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1 Tbsp</span> garam masala (15g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1 Tbsp</span> kosher salt (15g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1.5 lbs</span> boneless, skinless chicken thighs (680g)

## Curry Sauce

- <span class="ingredient-amount">2 Tbsp</span> vegetable oil (30g)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2-inch knob of ginger, finely diced
- 6 cloves of garlic, sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- <span class="ingredient-amount">2 tsp</span> paprika (8g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">2 tsp</span> ground cumin (9g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1 Tbsp</span> garam masala (14g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1.5 tsp</span> turmeric powder (6g)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">14 oz</span> crushed tomatoes
- <span class="ingredient-amount">2/3 cup</span> water (160ml)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">1 cup</span> heavy cream (240ml)
- <span class="ingredient-amount">2 Tbsp</span> unsalted butter (28g)
- Steamed rice
- Fresh cilantro

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the yogurt, garam masala, and kosher salt until combined.

2. Toss the chicken thighs with the marinade until fully coated. Cover and marinate for 30 minutes or overnight.

3. In a large pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat until shimmering hot.

4. Add chicken in batches and sear for 2-3 minutes on each side. Set aside once done.

5. Lower the heat to medium and add a touch more oil if needed. Add the diced onion, ginger, and garlic. Season with salt and pepper. Sauté for 2-3 minutes until softened.

6. Stir in the paprika, cumin, garam masala, and turmeric. Toast the spices for 1-2 minutes.

7. Mix in the crushed tomatoes and water. Simmer for 5-8 minutes until the sauce has reduced.

8. Return the seared chicken to the pan, ensuring it's covered with the sauce. Let it simmer for 3-5 minutes.

9. Stir in the heavy cream until the mixture thickens. Bring to a light simmer before reducing heat to low. Simmer for 3-4 minutes, stirring often.

10. Turn off heat and stir in the butter until melted. Season to taste with salt.

11. Serve in a shallow bowl over steamed rice and top with fresh cilantro.
